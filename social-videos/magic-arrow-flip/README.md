# The Magic Flipping Arrow — Grade 4 science short

A vertical (9:16) explainer video for **YouTube Shorts** and **Instagram Reels**.
Kids draw an arrow, put a clear cup of water in front of it, and the arrow flips
to point the other way. Then they use the same cup as a "spy decoder" for
secret words written backwards.

**Topic:** light and refraction (how a curved "lens" of water bends light).

## The experiment

**You need:** a sheet of paper, a marker, a clear round cup, and water.

**Safety (child-safe and home-safe):**
- Use a **clear plastic cup**, or a sturdy glass with a grown-up nearby.
- Keep a towel handy and wipe up spills straight away. Keep water away from electronics.
- There's no heat, no chemicals and nothing sharp. The only ingredient is tap water.

**Steps**
1. Draw a big, thick arrow pointing **left** on the paper, and stand the paper up (tape it to a wall or lean it on a book).
2. Put the **empty** cup about **10–15 cm (a hand-length) in front of the paper**. Look through it from about an arm's length away, at eye level. Nothing happens yet.
3. **Slowly** pour water into the cup until the water is above the arrow. The arrow now points **right**!
4. **Spy trick:** write a short word **backwards** (mirror writing). An easy way: write the word on thin paper, flip the sheet over, and trace the letters through from the back. Put it behind the cup of water and the word reads correctly.

**If it doesn't flip:** move the paper a little farther from the cup, make sure the cup is round with smooth sides and no pattern, and fill the water higher than the arrow.

### What's happening (notes for teachers and parents)
- Light travels in straight lines. When it passes from air into water (and back out), it **bends**. This is called **refraction**.
- A round cup of water is curved from side to side, so it acts like a **lens**. It bends light from the left side of the drawing across to the right, and light from the right across to the left. The rays **cross over**, so the picture comes out reversed.
- It only flips **left↔right**, not up↔down, because the cup is curved sideways but straight up and down.
- The flip only happens when the paper is **far enough behind** the cup (beyond the "focal point"). If the paper is very close, the water just makes the arrow look bigger. This makes a great mini-investigation (see below).
- **Your eye has a lens too.** It makes an upside-down picture on the retina, and your brain turns it the right way up.

### Extensions: turn it into an investigation
- **Two arrows:** draw one arrow near the top of the paper and one near the bottom, then fill the cup only halfway. Only the arrow behind the water flips. That proves the water is doing it. (This is the picture on the cover.)
- **Find the flip point:** slowly slide the paper closer to and farther from the cup. Where does the arrow flip? Measure the distance with a ruler.
- **Round vs. square:** try a square container with flat sides. Does the arrow flip? (No. Flat sides don't act as a lens.)
- **Word hunt:** which words look the same after flipping? Try `WOW`, `TOOT` and `MOM`, then work out why.

**Curriculum links:** this supports NGSS **4-PS4-2** (a model of how light that reflects off objects enters the eye so we can see them). It also practises *Developing and Using Models* and *Planning and Carrying Out Investigations*.

## Rebuild or edit

Everything is generated from code, so nothing is stock footage or stock audio.

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `src/script_lines.py` | Narration text, one entry per line |
| `src/narration.py` | Voice-over with [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) (offline neural TTS, voice `af_heart`), plus word timings from forced alignment |
| `src/render.py` | Timeline, the nine animated scenes, burned-in captions, cover and SRT (skia-python) |
| `src/engine.py` | Drawing toolkit and props: paper, arrow, cup of water (mirrored and magnified behind the waterline), jug, mascot |
| `src/audio.py` | Synthesised background music and sound effects, mixed with ducking under the voice |
| `src/build.sh` | Runs the whole pipeline and loudness-normalises the audio to −14 LUFS |

```bash
cd social-videos/magic-arrow-flip/src
pip install -r requirements.txt           # plus: ffmpeg, fonts-noto-color-emoji, libegl1
python3 prepare_voice_model.py            # one-time: Kokoro model + voices (~120 MB download)
./build.sh                                # writes the .mp4, cover.jpg and captions.srt one level up
```

**Changing the words:** edit `script_lines.py` and run `./build.sh`. Every scene is timed to the spoken words, so the visuals re-sync automatically.
**Changing the voice:** set `KOKORO_VOICE`, e.g. `KOKORO_VOICE=af_bella ./build.sh`, and adjust the speed with `KOKORO_SPEED`.

**Credits and licences:** voice by Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) via `kokoro-onnx`. Font: Outfit (SIL OFL, included in `src/fonts`). Emoji: Noto Color Emoji (SIL OFL). Music and sound effects are synthesised in `audio.py`.
