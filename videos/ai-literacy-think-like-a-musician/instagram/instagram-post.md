## Settings when posting

| Setting | Choose | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cover | Upload `reel-cover.jpg` ("Add from camera roll") | The first frame of the video is a half-filled grid. The cover shows the title. |
| **AI label** (Advanced settings) | **On** | Meta asks creators to label realistic-sounding AI audio, and the narration is a synthetic voice. |
| Auto-generated captions | Off | The captions are already burned into the video, so Instagram's would appear twice. |
| Audio name | Rename the original audio to "Think Like a Musician – TomoClub" | All music and sound effects are original, so there's nothing to license. |
| Don't add Instagram music | – | Extra tracks would be mixed over the narration. |
| Tag people / location | None | No students are identifiable, and nobody should be tagged. |
| Comments → Hidden words | Turn on "Hide comments" and add custom words: phone, address, snapchat, whatsapp, school | Catches comments that try to share contact details. |
