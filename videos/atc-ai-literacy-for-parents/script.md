# Narration script

Written in Varchas's register (short lines, a named tool in the first lines, "Well," once, "isn't it?" once, one fragment run, British spelling, no em dashes). It uses "we" for All Things Classroom because the narrator is a synthetic voice and shouldn't speak as Varchas. Varchas only speaks in the real class clips.

To swap in Varchas's own voice, record each line as `vo/<id>.wav` (the ids are in `build/long.py` and `build/reel.py`), update `vo/durations.json`, and re-run the audio, scenes and final steps. Then drop the "made with AI" line from the outro.

### 1:18 · Habit 1 (continued)

> Something to anticipate. That's the whole trick. An AI writes one small piece at a time, always picking the most likely next one.
>
> Try it at home. Open Song Maker together - it's free, in the browser - build three bars, and let your child guess the fourth. Then swap.
>
> AI goes for the safe note every time. Notice how often your child doesn't.

### 1:40 · Habit 2: Play It Twice

> Two. Play it twice.
>
> Musicians replay a tricky bit to check it holds up. Ask an AI a question, then open a brand-new chat and ask it again.
>
> If a name, a number or a date changes, it was guessing. Give your child a word for that: a rumour, until you check it.

### 1:58 · Habit 3: Flip the Key

> Three. Flip the key.
>
> Take a song from a major key to a minor key, and the whole mood changes. Questions work the same way.
>
> Ask: why is homework good for kids? Then, in a new chat: why is homework bad for kids?
>
> If it happily agrees both times, it's following your lead. Neutral questions get better answers, like: what are the pros and cons?

### 2:21 · Habit 4: Find the Sheet Music

> Four. Find the sheet music.
>
> Before your child repeats an AI fact - in a project, in class, at the dinner table - find the receipt.
>
> A real book or a site you trust, and the exact line that backs it up. No receipt? Don't repeat it.

### 2:36 · Habit 5: Play It By Ear

> Five. Play it by ear.
>
> A musician who can only play while reading the sheet hasn't learned the song yet.
>
> So after AI helps with homework, close the tab and ask your child to explain it out loud in 30 seconds.
>
> Wherever they get stuck is the part they still need to learn.
