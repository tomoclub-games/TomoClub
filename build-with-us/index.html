Things leaders ask before writing

Fair questions.

Is there a cost for the initial project outline?

No. It's free and yours to keep. Budget ranges are in it so there are no surprises later.

Do we have to use one of your programs?

No. If one fits, we'll say so because it's faster and cheaper for you. If not, we scope something new.

What if it has nothing to do with AI?

Bring it anyway. Assessment design, special education supports, family engagement and teacher training are exactly where a psychologist, a curriculum designer and an engineer in the same room earn their keep.

How do districts usually fund this?

Title II and IV, state career-readiness or workforce grants, foundation grants, IDEA for special-education work, and reallocation from PD or curriculum lines. The outline names the routes we think apply, and we itemise curriculum and training separately because funders ask for it.

Who actually does the work?

Our own team, led by the co-founders. We don't hand projects to subcontractors.
