## LinkedIn post

What happens when kids get no rules?

We tried it in a live TomoClub Life Skills class. Our coach VK opened with: "We don't give you any rules. You have to talk to each other and figure it out."

Here's what happened over the next hour:

→ One student said: "I'll just be at the button unlocking the doors, you explore through each door." They split roles without being told to.
→ The challenges were hard on purpose, because that's how kids learn.
→ They won by working together. After every game they ran a short after-action review: what was supposed to happen, what actually happened, and what to change next time.
→ In one student's words: "Usually in other games you are competing against someone, but here you are actually working together for a certain goal."

And they rated the session 10 out of 10.

This is game-based SEL. Kids think they're just playing. Meanwhile they're practising communication, collaboration and resilience they can use at school, in sport and in life.
