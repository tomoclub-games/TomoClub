# Parent highlight: game-based learning (TAICY 2026, Round 2)

A roughly 3-minute, 16:9 (1920×1080) highlight cut from the 65-minute Zoom recording of TAICY Round 2.
It's for parents. It shows what a live TomoClub game-based session looks like, lets the students speak for

## Story

1. **Cold open.** VK: *"We don't give you any rules. You have to talk to each other and figure it out."* A student: *"Communication is key."*
2. **Title.** Game-based learning, live and unscripted.
3. **01 Think it through** (Game 1, a territory strategy game): the facilitator's questions, Vedant's strategy,
   Sudeep admitting he was lost, and VK turning that into a lesson on bouncing back.
4. **02 Win as a team** (Game 2, a co-op puzzle): splitting roles, then a Socratic exchange on what
   "communicating efficiently" means, ending on *"communication is a two-way street."*
5. **03 Reflect & level up.** The students replay a level with the lights off and clear it in 45 seconds
   because they remember the map. VK frames it as "embracing the chaos."
6. **04 In their own words.** Both students on what they enjoyed and learned. VK: *"The whole point of
   game-based learning is you figure out the rules."*
7. **Learning outcomes.** Strategic thinking, communication, collaboration, resilience, reflection.
8. **End card (12 s).** "Build the child AI can't replace." Then the CTA button, URL, QR code, email, phone and socials.

## Render

```bash

mkdir -p source && cp "/path/to/TAICY Round 2.mp4" source/
python3 render.py --source "source/TAICY Round 2.mp4"
```

`python3 render.py --placeholder` renders the full cut with stand-in frames for layout review. `--only id1,id2` re-renders
single segments. Read `out/*.edit-report.txt` for where each cut was snapped and how much gain each clip got.

To change the cut, edit `edl.json`. Each clip has source `in`/`out` seconds, caption `text` (`|` forces a caption
break), `role`, `speaker` and `chapter`. Graphics live in `graphics.py`.
