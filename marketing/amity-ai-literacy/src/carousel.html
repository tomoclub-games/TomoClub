What class looked like

Every session, students built, tested or argued.

01
Trained their own image classifiers
in Teachable Machine, using their own faces
02
Played Semantris
to see how a language model connects words
03
Audited their own TikTok feeds
What has the algorithm decided about me?
04
Took on an ethics question every week
Privacy, ownership, bias, who benefits and who pays

Taught by Amity's own teachers, not computer science specialists.
