This is a seed entry used to validate the `articles` content collection schema and the `/articles/[slug]` dynamic route during Phase 1 of the Astro migration. Real article content (21 existing pieces) still lives as static HTML under `public/articles/` and will be converted to this collection format in Phase 2.

## Why this exists

Content collections need at least one real entry for `astro check`/`astro build` to validate the schema and for the dynamic route to have something to render.
