Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 5, 2026 | Last Updated: May 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tomorrow Innovations Inc. ("TomoClub," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information about you when you visit our website at www.tomoclub.org (the "Website"), purchase our products or services, or interact with our communications. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly and transparently.

BY USING THE WEBSITE, YOU CONSENT TO THE DATA PRACTICES DESCRIBED IN THIS PRIVACY POLICY. IF YOU DO NOT AGREE, PLEASE DISCONTINUE USE OF THE WEBSITE.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect information you provide voluntarily when you interact with our Website, including when you:

  • Subscribe to our email newsletter or marketing list — we collect your name and email address;
  • Make a purchase — we collect billing name, billing address, email address, and payment information (processed and stored by our payment processor; TomoClub does not store full payment card data);
  • Complete a contact form or inquiry — we collect your name, email address, and message content;
  • Register for a webinar, event, or workshop — we collect registration details including name, email, school or institution affiliation;
  • Communicate with us via email — we collect the content of those communications.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Website, certain information is automatically collected through cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies, including:

  • IP address and general geographic location (country, state, city);
  • Browser type and version, operating system, and device type;
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and navigation patterns;
  • Referring URL (the website that directed you to ours);
  • Date and time of your visit;
  • Clicks, scroll depth, and other engagement metrics;
  • Advertising interactions and conversion events (via Meta/Facebook Pixel).

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third-party sources, including advertising platforms and analytics providers, which may be combined with information we already hold about you. We may also receive information from schools or institutions that purchase our products on behalf of educators or students.

1.4 Children Under 13

The Website is not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Our educational products may be used by students (including minors) as end-users, but all personal data is provided by adults — parents, guardians, or educational institutions — on their behalf. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data to us without your consent, please contact us immediately at info@tomoclub.org and we will delete such data promptly.

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

2.1 To Operate and Deliver Services

  • Process and fulfill purchases, memberships, and one-time product orders;
  • Deliver purchased digital products, ebooks, and course materials;
  • Manage event and webinar registrations;
  • Respond to inquiries, support requests, and communications;
  • Send transactional emails (order confirmations, receipts, delivery notices).

2.2 To Communicate with You

  • Send email newsletters, educational content, announcements, and promotional materials (with your consent or as permitted by law);
  • Notify you of changes to our products, services, or these policies;
  • Send invitations to events, webinars, and community activities.

2.3 To Improve Our Services

  • Analyze Website usage and traffic patterns via Google Analytics;
  • Understand user preferences and behavior to improve content and user experience;
  • Conduct market research and measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns;
  • Develop and test new features, products, and services.

2.4 For Marketing and Advertising

  • Serve targeted advertisements via Meta (Facebook) Pixel and similar tools;
  • Measure and optimize the performance of our advertising campaigns;
  • Retarget Website visitors with relevant advertisements on third-party platforms.

2.5 For Legal and Compliance Purposes

  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal obligations;
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions and other agreements;
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
  • Respond to lawful requests from courts, law enforcement, and regulatory authorities.

3. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING (U.S. PRIVACY LAW)

Under applicable U.S. privacy laws, our processing of your personal information is based on one or more of the following:

  • Contractual necessity — processing required to fulfill your purchase or deliver requested services;
  • Legitimate interests — processing for analytics, security, fraud prevention, and business operations where our interests are not overridden by your rights;
  • Consent — processing for marketing emails and non-essential cookies, where we have obtained your consent;
  • Legal obligation — processing required to comply with applicable law.

4. COOKIES AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

4.1 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. We use cookies and similar technologies (web beacons, pixels, local storage) to enhance your experience, analyze usage, and serve targeted advertising.

4.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies — Required for the Website to function. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies — Used by Google Analytics to collect aggregated data about Website traffic and user behavior to help us improve our Website.
  • Marketing and Advertising Cookies — Used by Meta/Facebook Pixel to track conversions, build advertising audiences, and measure the effectiveness of our ads.
  • Functional Cookies — Used to remember your preferences and settings.

4.3 Your Cookie Choices

You may control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may impact the functionality of the Website. You may also opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. To opt out of Meta advertising cookies, visit your Facebook Ad Preferences.

By continuing to use our Website after being presented with this Privacy Policy, you consent to our use of non-essential cookies. You may withdraw your consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings or contacting us.

5. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with third parties only in the following circumstances:

5.1 Service Providers

We share data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the Website and delivering Services, strictly for the purposes described in this Policy:

  • Google Analytics — website usage analytics (Google LLC, USA);
  • Meta / Facebook Pixel — advertising and conversion tracking (Meta Platforms, Inc., USA);
  • Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) — email marketing and transactional email delivery;
  • Mailchimp (Intuit Inc.) — email newsletter management and distribution;
  • Zoho Corporation — CRM, business operations, and customer management;
  • Payment processor(s) — secure payment processing (specific processor disclosed at checkout).

All service providers are contractually obligated to use your data only as necessary to perform services for us and in compliance with applicable law.

5.2 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, sale of all or substantially all of our assets, reorganization, or bankruptcy, your personal information may be transferred to the successor entity. We will notify you of any such transfer and any material changes to this Privacy Policy.

5.3 Legal Compliance and Protection

We may disclose your information to third parties when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to:

  • Comply with a subpoena, court order, legal obligation, or lawful government request;
  • Protect and defend the rights, property, or safety of TomoClub, our users, or the public;
  • Investigate or prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents;
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions.

5.4 Aggregated and De-Identified Data

We may share aggregated or de-identified data that cannot reasonably be used to identify you with third parties for research, analytics, or business purposes.

6. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Specifically:

  • Email marketing data is retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion;
  • Purchase and transaction records are retained for a minimum of seven (7) years for accounting, tax, and legal compliance purposes;
  • Analytics data collected via Google Analytics is retained per Google's standard data retention settings, which we configure to 26 months;
  • Inquiry and communication records are retained for three (3) years from the date of last communication;
  • Cookie data is retained in accordance with individual cookie lifespans disclosed in cookie settings.

Upon expiration of the applicable retention period, we securely delete or anonymize personal information.

7. DATA SECURITY

TomoClub implements commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data transmitted between your browser and the Website;
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis;
  • Use of PCI-compliant payment processors for all financial transactions;
  • Regular assessment of security practices and procedures.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that creates a risk of harm to you, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

8. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

8.1 All Users

Regardless of your location, you have the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • Access — You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correction — You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information;
  • Deletion — You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal obligations and legitimate business needs;
  • Opt-Out of Marketing — You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the "unsubscribe" link or by contacting info@tomoclub.org;
  • Withdraw Consent — Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

8.2 California Residents — CCPA/CPRA Rights

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), including:

  • Right to Know — The right to know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell;
  • Right to Delete — The right to request deletion of your personal information;
  • Right to Correct — The right to request correction of inaccurate personal information;
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing — We do not sell personal information. We do share data with advertising partners (e.g., Meta Pixel) which may constitute "sharing" under CPRA. You may opt out by contacting us;
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information — You may direct us to limit the use of sensitive personal information;
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA/CPRA rights.

To submit a verifiable consumer request under the CCPA/CPRA, contact us at info@tomoclub.org with "California Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 45 days, with a possible extension of an additional 45 days when reasonably necessary.

8.3 Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, and Other State Residents

Residents of Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), Texas (TDPSA), and other states with comprehensive privacy laws may have rights similar to those described above, including rights of access, correction, deletion, portability, and opt-out of targeted advertising. To exercise any such rights, contact us at info@tomoclub.org.

8.4 How to Submit a Request

To exercise any of your privacy rights, please contact us at info@tomoclub.org. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request. We will not charge a fee for processing your request unless your request is excessive, repetitive, or manifestly unfounded.
