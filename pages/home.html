Fair questions

What leaders ask before they write.

What does it cost?

Every project is scoped after a 20-minute discovery call, because no two districts need the same thing. The first outline is free and yours to keep, and it includes a budget range so there are no surprises.

How do districts usually fund this work?

Common routes are Title II and Title IV, state career-readiness or workforce grants, IDEA for special education work, Perkins V for CTE outcomes, foundation grants, and reallocating PD or curriculum lines. We itemise curriculum and training separately because funders ask for it.

Do we have to use one of your programs?

No. If an existing program fits, we will say so, because it is faster and cheaper for you. If nothing fits, we scope something new with you. If we are not the right partner, we will tell you that too.

What if our problem has nothing to do with AI?

Bring it anyway. Assessment design, special education supports, family engagement and teacher training are exactly where a psychologist, a curriculum designer and an engineer in the same room are useful.

How do you handle student data?

TomoClub is SOC 2 and GDPR compliant, and student names and student data are never made public. We walk through how data is handled for your specific project on the first call.

Who actually does the work?

Our own team, led by the co-founders. We do not hand projects to subcontractors.
