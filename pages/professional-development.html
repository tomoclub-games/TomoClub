The Curriculum

The Content Road Map.

Personalized to your teachers' needs post AI readiness survey. Immediate Application.

Level 1: AI PD Fundamentals

Module Focus Details & Objectives
1 Awareness Foundations of AI Introduces AI in a safe, non-intimidating sandbox environment. Addresses common misconceptions and builds confidence.
2 Efficiency Smart Workflows & Time Savings Educators learn smart prompting to create lesson plans, quizzes, and differentiated content. Automates tasks to save 8+ hours per week.
3 Ethics & Assessment Responsible Use & Academic Integrity Focuses on ethical use, identifying bias and "hallucinations." Provides strategies to prevent misuse and maintain academic integrity.
4 Personalization Differentiated Instruction Educators learn how AI can instantly customize assignments for diverse learners, supporting English language learners, advanced students, and students with learning differences.
The Next Level

Level 2: AI PD Advanced.

Level 2 equips your staff to move from AI awareness to AI authorship — building real tools, workflows, and confidence. Every module is hands-on, school-relevant, and immediately actionable. No coding background required.

5 Module 5 60 min

AI for Deep Research & Sense-Making

  • Research vs search — how AI synthesises while Google retrieves
  • NotebookLM advanced — multi-doc querying across curriculum & standards
  • Gemini Deep Research — multi-step synthesis with report output
  • Trust & verification — when to follow, when to challenge AI outputs
Tools NotebookLM · Perplexity · Gemini Deep Research · Claude Projects
6 Module 6 90 min

Understanding & Building AI Agents

  • What agents actually are — beyond chatbots: memory, tools, autonomous action
  • Agent anatomy — trigger → perceive → decide → act → loop
  • Gemini in Sheets — sidebar AI, formula generation, plain-English analysis
  • Ethics of autonomy — what agents should never decide alone
Tools Gemini in Sheets · Gemini in Docs · Google AppScript + AI · Zapier
7 Module 7 90 min

Designing AI Workflows

  • Workflow vs agent — one task vs a chain of connected tasks
  • Chaining LLMs — output of prompt 1 becomes input of prompt 2
  • Branching logic — if/else decisions inside a pipeline
  • Human-in-the-loop checkpoints — designing for trust, not speed
Tools Make.com · Google AppScript · Gemini in Sheets · n8n (optional)
8 Module 8 75 min + 15 min wrap

Building Apps Without Code

  • What makes a good teacher-built app — one job, zero training needed
  • Bolt.new — describe your app in plain English, get a prototype in minutes
  • Glide — turn a Google Sheet into a mobile app, free for educators
  • Connecting S5-S8: knowledge base + agent + workflow = app backend
Tools Bolt.new · Glide (free for edu) · Google Sites + AppScript · Gamma
