The Problem

Students are already using AI. Very few understand it.

Without a structured curriculum, students pick up habits and shortcuts without developing the judgment to use these tools responsibly.

Academic integrity issues

Over-reliance without critical thinking. A generation that is dependent on AI without understanding it.

School leaders feel the pressure

The question is how to act on it.

The Solution

A curriculum built for how students actually learn.

Two differentiated tracks designed to meet students exactly where they are.

Middle School Track

Grades 6–8

Foundational AI literacy. What AI is, how it learns, what it can and cannot do, and how to use it safely and ethically.

High School Track

Grades 9–12

Applied AI literacy. How AI systems work, real-world case studies, ethical frameworks, and project-based learning that connects AI to future careers.

What Students Do

Inside each unit

AI Foundations & Big Ideas

WKS 1–5
  • Explore AI history, definitions, and real-world examples
  • Compare rule-based logic vs machine learning
  • Train models using Teachable Machine

Generative AI & Prompting

WKS 6–10
  • Craft and refine prompts for LLMs and image generators
  • Co-create stories and analyse AI hallucinations
  • Explore tools: ChatGPT, Gemini, Canva, DALL·E

Ethics & Digital Citizenship

WKS 11–15
  • Identify bias, fairness, and privacy issues in AI
  • Critique deepfakes and AI-driven misinformation
  • Develop personal and class AI usage policies

AI in Careers & Domains

WKS 16–20
  • Explore AI in healthcare, journalism, education, business
  • Hear from industry experts on AI career pathways
  • Analyse real-world AI case studies across sectors

Capstone Lab

WKS 21–30
  • Identify a real-world problem and design an AI solution
  • Build prototypes using no-code tools
  • Present findings at a public project showcase
