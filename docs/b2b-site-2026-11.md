## 2. Audit findings that drove the rebuild (live site, 17 Nov 2026)

1. The home page was the parents (B2C) page: "Book a free class", INR prices, and six unfinished video placeholders showing on the live page. The B2B pitch sat at /schools behind a small menu link. The home page gets the most search clicks (338 of 545 in the Search Console baseline).
2. Parents pages, booking code and "For Parents" links were across the site, including a "Go to parents program" button at the top of /schools.
3. Every B2B button led to a general contact form (Tally) or "Request a pilot". Build With Us, the strongest offer, was only in the menu.
4. Forms: the Build With Us form posted straight to Apps Script with no spam protection (3 bot rows in the sheet). Nothing reached Instantly.
5. Claims did not match the approved figure ("33 districts / 10k+ students"): 10,000+ / 15,000+ students, 5,000+ teachers, 12+ or 14+ US states, 10+ countries. Results page said "28 educators" for Amity PD; the case study says 40 trained and 28 survey responses. Network-wide "97% engagement" and "85% referral reduction" named no program or source. "Saves 8+ hours per week" had no source.
6. An expired "E-book going live May 1st" banner was in the HTML of 108 pages.
7. The About page used an AI-generated founders image with a visible "Grok" watermark.

## 3. What changed (branch `b2b-site`)

### New home page (`pages/home.html`, builds to `index.html`)
Inspired by the scroll storytelling reel: one request is followed from the first sentence to the classroom, with a camera-style zoom into the story and four pinned steps (20-minute call, prototype within 24 hours, product in about 3 months, support and reporting). Same theme as /schools: navy, teal, gold, crimson, Outfit, rounded cards, pill buttons, dark glass nav. Falls back to a stacked layout on phones and for reduced motion.

Sections: hero, partner logos, story, credentials, what we do (4 offers, Build With Us featured), results with sources, built with districts this year (with honest status labels), two real requests, three leader quotes, team, resources, FAQ (cost, funding, data, who does the work), start form plus Calendly.

### Archived B2C
Moved to `archive/b2c-2026-11/` (not deployed): parents home page, /life-skills, /ai-lab, /both-programs, parents-site.js, api/book.js, slots.js, collect.js, geo.js. 301 redirects to `/` for those URLs and for `/schools`. The old /schools page is in `archive/superseded-2026-11/`.

### Lead capture and Instantly
| Form or action | Endpoint | Sheet tab | Instantly intent | Suggested target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home, contact and Build With Us forms | /api/lead `discovery_request` | School leads | discovery_request | list (no emails; a co-founder replies by hand, as the form promises) |
| Calendly booking completed | /api/lead `call_booked` (details read from Calendly) | School leads | call_booked | list |
| Guide download | /api/signup `resource` | Toolkit downloads | guide_download | nurture campaign |
| Leaders of Tomorrow ebook | /api/signup `ebook_request` | E-book requests | ebook_download | nurture campaign |
| High-intent activity by a known download contact (book call click, case study, Build With Us or Results visit, cost FAQ, form abandon) | /api/event | Website events | high_intent | follow-up campaign |

People who asked to talk (discovery form or booked call) are never put into an automated sequence. A person is only ever in one Instantly campaign at a time (`skip_if_in_campaign`).

Spam protection on every form: hidden honeypot, minimum 3 seconds to submit, link-count check, per-IP rate limit, origin check, and Cloudflare Turnstile when its keys are set.

## 4. Go-live steps (in order)

1. Instantly: create lists "Website: discovery requests" and "Website: booked calls"; campaigns "Website: guide nurture", "Website: ebook nurture", "Website: high-intent follow-up" (write their emails before activating). Create an API key with `leads:create`.
2. Vercel env vars (Production and Preview):
   - `INSTANTLY_API_KEY`
   - `INSTANTLY_ROUTES` = `{"discovery_request":"list:<id>","call_booked":"list:<id>","guide_download":"campaign:<id>","ebook_download":"campaign:<id>","high_intent":"campaign:<id>"}`

   - `LEAD_TOKEN_SECRET` (any long random string)
   - `CALENDLY_TOKEN` must include the `scheduled_events:read` scope (it already exists for the old trial booking)
   - Optional: `TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY`, plus the site key in `assets/js/b2b-events.js`
3. Apps Script (form sheet): paste the updated `data-capture/Code.gs`, then Deploy > Manage deployments > Edit > New version (keeps the same URL). Adds the "School leads" and "Website events" tabs and Slack alerts for school leads.
4. GA4: mark lead_submitted, book_call_scheduled and resource_download as key events. LinkedIn: create conversions and paste their IDs into `assets/js/b2b-events.js`.
5. Merge, then test on the live site: send one form, book one test call, download one guide, and check the Sheet rows, Instantly list or campaign, Slack alert, and that /schools, /life-skills, /ai-lab and /both-programs redirect to /.
6. Request indexing for / in Search Console. The coverage checks already scheduled for 25 Nov and 9 Dec will show the effect.
