Top AI Tools to Help Teachers Assess Student Progress in 2025

Discover top AI tools teachers can use in 2025 to assess student progress, save time, and personalize learning effectively.

Introduction: What Schools Look Like Today – And Tomorrow

School leaders are currently immersed in planning next year’s goals and budgeting. However, one innovation — Artificial Intelligence — has the potential to completely transform the educational landscape. Brace for the rapid impact of this innovation by proactively staying ahead, rather than merely playing catch-up. Nothing has been this disruptive since the introduction of the internet.

What School Looks Like in 2025: A Mindset Shift

Today’s schools are no longer physical buildings with blackboards and paper tests. In 2025, what a “school” is has evolved through the eyes of students, teachers, and parents. For students, education occurs both in and out of classrooms — on computers, in online forums, and through adaptive digital lessons. Parents, on the other hand, desire real-time feedback, not term-end report cards. Teachers, in turn, are torn between lesson planning, differentiated learning requirements, and ongoing assessments — all while trying to keep up with technology. Post-COVID and now post-AI, it’s evident that education has reached a new era. Schools need to adopt AI in education — that is, to revolutionize how student development is monitored to remain future-proof.

Why AI in Education Matters More Than Ever

Artificial Intelligence is revolutionizing every big industry — finance to healthcare. Education is not behind. The bright spot? AI for teachers is not about replacing teachers. Instead, it’s like having another brain — helping with time-consuming tasks and improving decision-making. AI can do more than just generate content. Used to its full potential, it can identify areas for learning, give immediate feedback, and recommend learning strategies based on student profiles. Teachers, as a result, gain not only efficiency but also transparency when they use AI in the classroom.

Reimagining Student Assessment with AI

Historically, assessment meant paper, pencil, and grading hours. But today’s students need more than grades. They need timely, personalized feedback and the capacity to reflect and grow. AI in student assessment makes that a possibility — with fewer hours from educators. With intelligent tools, evaluation is dynamic and adaptive. Educators can observe trends, identify individual challenges, and assist students quicker. Simply put, AI enables the shift from “one-size-fits-all” to “what-this-student-needs-now.”

Real-World Applications of AI in the Classroom

Let’s take a step back and reflect on how AI is already saving teachers time and enhancing results:

  • Instant Writing Feedback: Instant feedback regarding organization, clarity, and grammar is given to the students. This enables the teachers to spend more time motivating higher-order thinking.
  • Unpacking Error Patterns: AI identifies where and why a student is becoming stuck in math or science and suggests specific interventions.
  • Tracking Reading Fluency: Tools now highlight student recordings and evaluate fluency, rate, and tone — automatically tracking progress week by week.

Having that in mind, let’s discuss the top AI tools for educators in 2025 that are revolutionizing tests.

Best AI Tools for Teachers to Evaluate Student Progress in 2025

Each of these technologies offers real-world applications that are already being used within classrooms. Here’s why they succeed:

Gradescope (by Turnitin)

Gradescope is widely used for AI-driven student grading.

  • Auto-Grading for Efficiency: Teachers upload assignments that are graded within minutes. Similar responses are grouped together, preventing duplicated feedback.
  • Data Dashboards: See class-wide errors and trends at one time. Quick reteaching of misinterpreted material is made easy.

Class Companion

Class Companion offers instant writing feedback.

  • Instant Writing Support: Students receive feedback on clarity, grammar, and coherence as they write.
  • Less Time Spent on Draft Reviews: More time is spent on critical thinking skills than on mechanics.

Edmentum Exact Path

Refining the monitoring of tailored math and reading instruction.

  • Adaptive Assessments: AI determines performance and generates individualized learning paths for every student.
  • Progress Monitoring: Teachers get feedback on progress, not on completion.

Conker.ai

Conker.ai is a quiz builder that’s easy and robust.

  • Rapid Quiz Generation: Teachers enter a topic and get instant, automatically scored quizzes.
  • Performance Insights: AI identifies which ideas are grasped effectively and should be retaught.

Sown to Grow

Sown to Grow emphasizes goal setting and reflection.

  • Student Reflections: Students monitor goals and reflect on their performance.
  • AI Patterns: AI recognizes patterns in effort and learning, notifying teachers of academic and emotional growth.
