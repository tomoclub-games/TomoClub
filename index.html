An adult teacher supervises every small group for the whole class. We do not sell your child's data and we do not use it for advertising.

Some sessions are recorded for internal quality review. Recordings are not shared publicly without parent consent.

What does the fee include?+

Each program is ₹2,999 a month (2026 offer; regular price ₹7,999) for four live 60-minute classes in a group of up to 12 with a coach. Both programs together are ₹4,999 a month for eight classes.

Getting started

How do I book?+

Tap Book my child's free class, choose your child's grade and program, then pick a time. Our team confirms your booking with you on WhatsApp.

What happens in the trial?+

Your child joins a live 60-minute class with a coach and a small group. It's free, and there is no obligation to continue.

What do I need for a class?+

A laptop, tablet or computer with a camera, microphone, keyboard, mouse and a stable internet connection. Our programs do not work on smartphones, by design.
