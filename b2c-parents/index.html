Good questions

Things parents usually ask

What grades is this for?

Grades 3 to 12. Your child is grouped with kids around the same age.

What does "accredited" mean?

STEM.org has reviewed and accredited every TomoClub program, and we hold their Best in STEM distinction (top 5%). You can verify both using the badges at the top of this page.

What happens in the free class?

A full 60-minute live class with a coach and a real group of kids. No credit card needed.

How much does it cost?

$99 a month per child for 4 live one-hour classes and a monthly progress report. Shown in your local currency.

What does my child need?

A laptop or desktop with a webcam, microphone and steady internet.

What language are classes taught in?

All classes are taught in English by our coaches. Kids who are comfortable following instructions in English do well, and the games make it easy to join in.

Are classes recorded?

No. Kids speak more freely that way. Coaches take notes for your monthly report instead.
